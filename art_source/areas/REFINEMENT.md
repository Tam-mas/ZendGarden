# Garden habitat refinement

The source entry point is `art_source/build_distinct_areas.py`. It installs `area_refinement.py` for the second construction pass and uses `area_botanicals.py` for all eighteen collection plants. Through the Blender MCP, call `build_specialties()` and then `build_one(index)` sequentially. Run `refine_area_bench.build()` for the shared bench. These write the Area libraries without clearing the user's active Blender scene. The refined bench has its own `overhaul/Area_Furnishings.blend` library and updates only the bench's reviewed candidate hash.

The eighteen plants use the established `botanical_geometry` and `botanical_detail` workflow: curved organs, mapped veins, normal/roughness textures and distinct anatomical growth stages. Ferns use fine black stipes and fan pinnules or divided tree-fern fronds with fibrous petiole bases and croziers. Other models include notched lily pads, iris standards and falls, orchid lips, wax-flower umbels, alpine rosettes and tubular dusk flowers. Reference anatomy: [RHS maidenhair](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/439/adiantum-aethiopicum/details), [RHS bird's nest fern](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/1728/asplenium-nidus/details), [ANBG soft tree fern](https://www.anbg.gov.au/gnp/interns-2003/dicksonia-antarctica.html), [SANBI water hawthorn](https://pza.sanbi.org/aponogeton-distachyos), [SANBI night phlox](https://pza.sanbi.org/zaluzianskya-capensis).

Decorative plants are exported as species anchors. `GardenAreaFlora` batches their detailed organs in per-area MultiMeshes, keeping functional canopy and dusk groups independent. Terrain anchors remain available for sculpting; refresh instance transforms after any terrain change. Living collection plants use the same detailed assets with separate growth-stage visibility, preserving their saved age and species.

Ground and walking collision still come from the exported terrain. Native habitat shaders blend shores and soil edges in world space. Water ribbons share continuous edges, longitudinal UVs and a gently graded surface. Fitted flagstones follow the path curve rather than the ground's material grid. Rocks have individually authored collision and footprint metadata; reservations keep them off planting pockets, existing botanical roots, stairs and curved paths. The asset check verifies root/stone clearance.

Orchard and kitchen soils use the established seven bed finishes. Ground partitions retain `area_bed_plot`, allowing finish changes to cover the authored soil while retaining its shape and sculpting. Orchard masks blend the soil margin into grass. Other habitats preserve their fixed terrain materials.

The four unmoved original free terrace containers are relocated from wall positions on load. Their object IDs and planted contents remain intact. Moved or purchased containers are retained. Glasshouse pot centres and soil elevations match the six collection slots. Shadecloth uses actual woven openings. Both stream bridge railings follow the deck arch; reedwater rails follow the boardwalk elevation.

Verification: `tests/check_areas.py`, `tests/areas.gd` in headless, Compatibility and Forward+, and the full `tests/run.sh`. Source libraries are checked separately through approved background Blender with `tests/check_blender_sources.py`. Whole-garden captures are in `captures/areas`; walking-height details are in `captures/area-refinement/details`. Review captures and test save files are separate from the personal garden.
