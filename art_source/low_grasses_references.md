# Low grasses and grass-like groundcovers

IDs 136–147 are append-only additions. Mature foliage stays between 10 and 30 cm;
spreading grasses use multiple connected tufts, while sedges and mondo grasses
have arching strap leaves. Seed spikes and mondo flowers remain small. Evergold
has cream centres with green edges; Snowline has thin white edges and green
centres. The low growth stages are authored in `low_grasses.blend`, alongside the
mature plants, and generated by `build_low_grasses.py` through Blender MCP or an
approved Blender background process. Rebuilding the larger growth library keeps
these separate, species-specific stages and their existing manifest entries.

Reference sources used for proportions, growth habits and colour:

- [RHS grass selection guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/types/grasses/ornamental/selection): Evergold, Snowline and compact grass shapes.
- [RHS sheep's fescue](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/7185/festuca-ovina/details): fine rolled leaves and compact tussocks.
- [RHS Snowline sedge](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/80433/carex-conica-snowline-v/details): small arching green leaves with white edges.
- [RHS dwarf golden sweet flag](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/166439/acorus-gramineus-minimus-aureus/details): dwarf yellow-green foliage for moist positions.
- [Kew bearskin fescue](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A972850-1/general-information): accepted botanical identity.
- [Missouri Botanical Garden grass collection](https://plantfinder.mobot.org/PlantFinderProfileResults.aspx?n=1&pt=11): buffalo grass's short growth, contrasted with taller prairie grasses.

The game represents foliage height and repeated small planting units rather than
the maximum height of every flowering culm or an entire untrimmed lawn. Its light
preferences simplify real horticultural requirements into the existing care system.
