The licence covers the project's first-party code, documentation and
original assets, including the original plant models, editable Blender
sources, generated textures and music. Independently licensed third-party
components keep their own licences. In particular, this does not change

development tools, fonts or libraries. Preserve their notices when
distributing a build.

Contributions are offered under the project's licence unless otherwise
agreed. Contributors retain their copyright; submitting a contribution
does not assign ownership or automatically permit later relicensing.

## Source available and free to play

This is a **source-available** project. It is not open source under the
[Open Source Initiative definition](https://opensource.org/osd), because
these terms restrict commercial use and charging users. The standard GNU
GPL allows selling copies, so it would not by itself meet the free-play
requirement. This licence is custom wording, not "GPL plus a restriction"
or a modified PolyForm licence.
