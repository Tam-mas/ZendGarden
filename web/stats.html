What the times mean

Game-open time is elapsed time after the garden loads, including background tabs, paused menus and device sleep.

Garden advancing counts time when the garden clock advances. It can include an unattended garden and excludes browser suspension and menus that pause the clock. It does not measure attention.

Reports arrive about every two minutes. Background browsers may delay them; time catches up on the next report. If a browser closes without sending a final report, the unreported tail is lost. Advancing time can miss up to five seconds at closing.

Sessions are separate visits, not unique people. No tab-focus tracking, names, save contents or persistent visitor identifiers are collected. Summaries expire after 90 days.
